# Search Keywords

Suche **englisch**. Pinterest-Ranking belohnt Stimmungswörter ("aesthetic", "editorial", "vintage") stärker als sachliche Begriffe.

## Query-Formel
`[Motiv] + [Stil/Medium] + [Stimmung/Ära] (+ [Farbe/Textur])`
z. B. `dollar bill crumpled black paper aesthetic`, `1950s grocery store photography`, `baroque oil painting white horse gold`

## Bausteine

**Stil/Medium:** editorial photography, cinematic still, film still 35mm, analog photography, kodachrome, polaroid, film grain, double exposure, surreal collage, photomontage, oil painting, baroque painting, renaissance art, vanitas, naive art, crayon drawing, linocut, woodcut, risograph, retro illustration, vintage advertisement, mid-century illustration, 1950s ad, zine collage, newspaper clipping

**Stimmung:** dark aesthetic, moody, chiaroscuro, nostalgic, melancholic, dystopian, opulent, decadent, minimalist, dreamy, eerie, old money, quiet luxury, brutalist

**Ära:** 1920s, 1950s, 1970s, 1980s, victorian, art deco, belle epoque, industrial revolution, great depression

**Textur:** crumpled paper, torn paper, film scratches, light leak, halftone, grainy, patina, craquelure, marble, gold leaf, velvet

**Wörtliche Anker:** money, dollar bill, cash, coins, gold bars, bank, safe, wallet, piggy bank, stock ticker, price tag, receipt, supermarket, grocery, gas station, apartment, villa, mansion, yacht, vintage car

## Query-Strategie
1. Pro Szene: **2 wörtliche + 2 metaphorische + 1-2 Texturqueries**.
2. Wenn Ergebnisse generisch: Ära wechseln, Medium wechseln (Foto → Gemälde), Textur ergänzen.
3. Negativ-Filter gedanklich anwenden: kein "stock photo", "3d render", "icon", "infographic", "clipart".
4. Dann **Pin-Detailseite** öffnen, "Ähnliche Pins" mitnutzen (oft die besten Treffer).
5. Auf Quellen-Domains ausweichen für klare Rechte: Wikimedia Commons, Met Museum Open Access, Rijksmuseum, Library of Congress, NYPL Digital Collections, Flickr Commons, Unsplash/Pexels (nur für Fotos, keine Kunststile).

## Suchlink-Format
`https://www.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=<url-encoded query>` (erzeugt von `scripts/build_search_links.py`). Das ist eine **Suchanfrage**, kein Fund.
